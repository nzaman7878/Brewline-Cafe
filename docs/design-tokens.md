## Color Mode
- **Dark mode** (primary)
- Color variant: **Tonal Spot** (depth with tonal variations)

## Design Principles
1. **Menu-first** — food imagery is the hero
2. **Speed & Convenience** — minimal clicks to order
3. **Dark & Warm** — premium cafe atmosphere
4. **Mobile-first** — responsive, optimized for phones
5. **Micro-animations** — subtle transitions enhance UX
